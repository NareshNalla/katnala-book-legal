Katnala Book / కట్నాల బుక్ — Delete account & data

Developer: Naresh Nalla
App: Katnala Book (Google Play)

This page explains how to delete some or all of your Katnala Book data, with or without deleting your account.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can remove data while staying signed in:

  1. Open Katnala Book and sign in.
  2. Delete individual gifts, guests, events, or reminders using the in-app delete/edit controls on those screens.
  3. Optional photos attached to a gift are removed when you delete that gift.

Deleted immediately in the app: the gift, guest, event, reminder, or photo you removed (and the synced Firebase copy of that item when sync is on).

Kept: your account (username/mobile, PIN, security question), profile fields you did not change, and any other gifts/guests/events you did not delete.

If you cannot delete something in the app, open a request:
Request data deletion (GitHub issue)
Include your username/mobile and what to delete. We aim to complete these within 30 days.

Delete your whole account and all associated data

  1. Open a deletion request here:
    Request account deletion (GitHub issue)
  2. In the issue body, include:
    • Your Katnala Book username or mobile number
    • The name used in the app (if any)
    • The words Please delete my Katnala Book account
  3. On your phone, also clear app data or uninstall Katnala Book (Android Settings → Apps → Katnala Book → Storage → Clear data, or Uninstall). That removes local data on the device immediately.

We aim to complete cloud-account deletion within 30 days of a valid request. You will get a reply on the GitHub issue when it is done.

What we delete for a full account request

  • Account credentials (username/mobile, PIN hash, security question/answer)
  • Profile fields (name, address)
  • Guests, gifts, events, reminders, and related notes/photos stored for your account
  • Synced copies in Firebase Realtime Database for that account (when sync was used)

What we may keep (and for how long)

  • Device-local data remains on a phone until you clear app data or uninstall — we cannot erase another person’s device remotely.
  • Deletion-request records (the GitHub issue and our reply) may be kept up to 12 months for proof that we handled your request.
  • Backups / logs that are not tied to a live account, if any, are removed on the normal backup rotation (typically within 90 days).
  • Data Google Play, AdMob, or Gemini process under their own terms is governed by those services; we delete what we control in the Katnala Book account and our Firebase sync for that account.

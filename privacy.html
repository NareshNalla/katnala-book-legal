Katnala Book / కట್నాల బుక್ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Katnala Book is a traditional gift ledger app for events such as weddings. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

Who we are

The app is published by Naresh Nalla (GitHub: NareshNalla). Contact via GitHub issues on the legal repo or the Play Store listing contact email.

Data the app stores

  • Account: username or mobile number, PIN hash, name, address, security question/answer
  • Guests: name, phone, village/place, group
  • Gifts: person, amount, item description, event, date, optional photo
  • Events and reminders
  • Device: language preference, notification setting

Gift amounts and guest lists are family financial records. Treat the device PIN as sensitive.

Where data lives

  • On the phone in a local database (Room)
  • If sync is enabled, on Firebase Realtime Database for backup across devices
  • Optional paper-ledger photos may be sent to Google Gemini only when you use Scan, to read names and amounts

What we do not do

  • We do not sell guest lists or gift amounts
  • We do not use gift data for advertising profiles
  • Ads, if shown later, will not be placed on the Add Gift screen

Sharing

Data leaves the device when you use cloud sync, Scan (Gemini), or when you share an invite/PDF yourself. Google Play, Firebase, and Gemini process data under their own terms when those features are used.

Retention and deletion

You can clear app data from Android settings or use in-app logout/reset where provided. Uninstalling removes local data. Cloud copies remain until you delete the synced account data or request deletion.

Children

The app is for adults managing family events. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page when collection practices change. The date at the top is the latest version.
